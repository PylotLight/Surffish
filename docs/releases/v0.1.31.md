# Surffish v0.1.31

Mini window New Tab pages get the browser New Tab's photo background and clock, and the menu bar popout's address field gets its own row so the tab strip stays visible.

> This release ships on an Electron **beta** (45.0.0-beta.6). Main tracks Electron betas; a daily check opens a bump PR when a new one is out.

## New

- **New Tab backgrounds in mini windows**: the mini New Tab page uses the same background photo as the browser New Tab (the cached Wikimedia photo, or one of the bundled offline photos when nothing is cached), with the photo credit at the bottom. Settings → **New Tab** applies here too: background dim and blur, icon size, labels on or off, and clock weight and tabular digits (#296)
- **Clock on the mini New Tab page**: shown when the New Tab clock is on, with the same time, date, 12/24 h, seconds and time zone settings. World clocks, greeting and weather stay on the browser New Tab only (#296)

## Fixed

- the menu bar popout no longer hides its tab strip while the address field is open: with tabs in the popout, the field now has its own row under the tab strip, and the dropdown hangs under that row. one-tab popouts and Float / Push / Overlay mini windows are unchanged (#296)
- the redundant "Search or type an address above · ⌘L" hint is gone from the mini New Tab page; a click on the page still brings the field back after Esc (#296)
- mini New Tab icon rows stay centred (a short last row too, at most six across), and private and empty states show as small pills on the photo (#296)

## Performance

- the mini New Tab page stays light: no renderer bundle and no backdrop blur, the photo is picked once per page, and the clock only ticks while the clock is on and the page is open (each second only with seconds on and the window shown) (#296)

## Packaging

- Apple silicon zip only, unsigned and not notarized. If macOS reports the app is damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Surffish.app` on a copy downloaded from the official release
