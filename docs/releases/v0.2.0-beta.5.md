# Surffish v0.2.0-beta.5

A focused follow-up to [beta.4](https://github.com/PylotLight/Surffish/releases/tag/v0.2.0-beta.4) fixes the New Tab handoff from the collapsed sidebar rail.

## Fixes

- **Collapsed-rail New Tab transition:** When New Tab is opened from the collapsed rail, the content surface now settles its sidebar morph instead of animating an outgoing panel ghost. This prevents a full-width tab label from flashing or overlapping the New Tab card while the rail is collapsed; the card keeps its normal width transition.

## Regression coverage

- The Electron smoke test now clicks New Tab from the collapsed rail and samples the visible handoff frame by frame, checking that panel labels do not paint while the rail is collapsed or beyond the card, and that no outgoing panel ghost appears.

## Dependency baseline

- Beta.4 introduced the continuous browser frame and documented the Electron and dependency updates. This focused follow-up changes no dependency versions; see the [beta.4 notes](https://github.com/PylotLight/Surffish/releases/tag/v0.2.0-beta.4) for that baseline.

## Packaging

- Expected asset: Apple silicon macOS ZIP, `Surffish-0.2.0-beta.5-mac-arm64.zip`. The app remains unsigned and not notarized; there are no Windows or Linux release assets.
