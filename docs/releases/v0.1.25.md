# Surffish v0.1.25

The menu bar popout finally behaves across desktops: click the icon on any Space and it drops in right there, and it stays put when you swipe between Spaces. Its slide in and out is smoother too. Tabs also keep their back/forward history across restarts. Ships on Electron 45.0.0-beta.5.

> This release ships on an Electron **beta** (45.0.0-beta.5). Main tracks Electron betas; a daily check opens a bump PR when a new one is out.

## New
- **Tab history survives restarts**: each tab's back/forward list (up to 50 entries) is saved with the session and restored after a relaunch, so Back works like before you quit. Private, Glance and Settings tabs are never saved (#270)

## Fixed
- **Menu bar popout on the right desktop**: with the browser window on another Space, clicking the menu bar icon now shows the popout on the Space you're on instead of the browser's Space. Applies to mini windows, their headers and edge handles too. Surffish is never activated for it (#271)
- **Popout stays up across Space swipes**: swiping to another desktop (or Ctrl+arrow) no longer flashes and hides the popout. Clicking another app still hides it straight away, ⌘Tab hides it after a moment, and Esc, the icon and Pin work as before (#272)

## Changed
- **Smoother popout motion**: opening is a short slide and fade with a softer ease-out; closing now fades back toward the icon instead of vanishing. Reopening mid-close turns back from where it is. Reduced motion stays instant (#272)

## Diagnostics
- The mini window debug log (⌘K **Start Logging Mini Window Header Events**, or `SURFFISH_DEBUG_HEADER=1`) now records Space placement, Space changes, blur decisions, tray clicks and native addon load problems (#271, #272)

## Packaging
- Apple silicon zip only, unsigned and not notarized. If macOS reports the app is damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Surffish.app` on a copy downloaded from the official release
