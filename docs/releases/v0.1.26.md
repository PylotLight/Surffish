# Surffish v0.1.26

Signing PDFs got a proper upgrade: pick your pen weight and ink, undo a stray stroke, choose a script font for typed signatures, or paste a photo of your signature and the paper background is cleaned away. Signatures now fit signature fields instead of landing huge and bold. PDFs from Gmail and other signed-in sites also open in the editor now instead of failing with a 401. Ships on Electron 45.0.0-beta.5.

> This release ships on an Electron **beta** (45.0.0-beta.5). Main tracks Electron betas; a daily check opens a bump PR when a new one is out.

## New
- **Pen weight and undo when drawing**: a 5-step weight slider (fine to marker) shows live on the pad and is saved with the signature. Undo Stroke (or ⌘Z on the pad) removes the last stroke (#276)
- **Natural-looking ink**: stroke width follows stylus pressure, or speed with a mouse or trackpad, with smoothing and tapered ends. Signatures are placed as crisp vector outlines, so they stay small in the PDF (#276)
- **Ink colours**: black ink, blue ballpoint or fountain blue, carried into the saved PDF (#276)
- **Signature size**: S, M or L sets the default placed size for signatures and initials (#276)
- **Font picker for typed signatures**: clickable previews of your name in installed script fonts, plus bundled Great Vibes and Allura, with a bold toggle (#276)
- **Image signatures**: paste from the clipboard, drag and drop onto the dialog, or pick a file. The paper background is removed, the image is cropped to the ink and recoloured to your chosen ink (#276)

## Fixed
- **Signature fields fit properly**: signatures sit inside the field with padding, keep their shape and are capped at your chosen size, so a tall box no longer gets a giant signature (#276)
- **Gmail and signed-in PDFs open in the editor**: attachments from Gmail and other cookie-protected PDFs no longer show "Could not download the PDF (HTTP 401)" in Edit mode. The editor reads the PDF from the open tab, and Back to viewer is instant for web PDFs (#274)

## Changed
- Your ink, size, weight and font choices are remembered. Existing saved signatures load exactly as before (#276)

## Packaging
- Apple silicon zip only, unsigned and not notarized. If macOS reports the app is damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Surffish.app` on a copy downloaded from the official release
- Bundled fonts Great Vibes and Allura are under the SIL Open Font License, listed in docs/third-party-assets.md (#276)
