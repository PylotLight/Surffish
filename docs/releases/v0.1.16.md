# Surffish v0.1.16

Mini windows can now live in the menu bar, every tab gets its own volume, and tucked minis peek out when you rest the pointer on their handle.

## New
- **Menu bar minis**: send a mini window (or a group) to the menu bar from the new header button, the header or edge handle right-click menu, ⌘K **Send Mini Window to Menu Bar**, or a tab's right-click **Open in Menu Bar**. They hide behind one fish icon, which shows a count with 2+ minis and ♪ while one is playing (#214)
- Click the fish icon and the mini drops in right under it. Click the icon again, press Esc or click away to hide it, or pin it with the new header pin button to keep it open. Hidden isn't closed: the page keeps running and audio keeps playing (#214)
- Right-click the icon to pick which menu bar mini shows, Pin, Return to Window, Move to Edge, open Mini Window Settings or Hide Menu Bar Icon (#214)
- **Menu bar icon** setting in Settings → General → Mini window: When in use (the default), Always or Never. With nothing in the menu bar and When in use, no icon is created and nothing extra loads (#214)
- Menu bar minis and their popout size come back after a restart. Private pages can go to the menu bar but are never saved (#214)
- **Per-tab volume**: hover the speaker on a tab in the sidebar or a split pane and a 0-100% slider slides out. Click still mutes, the scroll wheel moves it ±5%. The speaker now also shows while a tab holds media or is muted (#212)
- The same speaker and slider are on mini window tab chips, and in the header next to the title for a single-tab mini (#212)
- ⌘K **Volume Up** / **Volume Down** in 10% steps (#212)
- Volume is remembered per site, like zoom. Private tabs use the saved level but never save their own. Settings → Privacy → **Tab volume** lists sites and resets one or all, and clearing zoom and volume levels or forgetting a site resets it too (#212)
- **Peek on hover**: rest the pointer on a tucked mini's edge handle and it slides out for a look, then tucks back when you move away. Passing over the handle doesn't trigger it. Stacked minis on that edge peek together (#211)
- Click or type inside a peeked mini to pin it out, like a normal slide out (#211)
- Peek is on by default, with a hover delay of 200, 350 (default), 600 or 1000 ms in Settings → General → Mini window, and a ⌘K toggle (#211)

## Improved
- **Smoother mini window motion**: tucks, slide outs, peeks, stack slides, handle drags and header drags all run on one loop paced to your display's refresh rate, so the window, its floating header, its handle and stacked followers move in the same frame (#211)
- Handles making room for each other now glide instead of jumping, and an interrupted slide carries on from where it got to instead of snapping (#211)

## Known limits
- No hover peek from the menu bar icon, and the menu bar popout can't be moved or docked. A Float above header switches to Push there, since there's no room above the menu bar (#214)
- Tab volume doesn't reach audio a site plays only through its own Web Audio graph that never connects to the speakers (rare), or audio in workers (#212)
- An extension reading a page's native media volume sees the tab-scaled value (#212)

## CI
- New smoke tests: `test:electron-mini-window-menubar` (#214), `test:electron-tab-volume` (#212), `test:electron-mini-window-peek` and `test:electron-mini-window-motion`, which checks frame gaps, one move per window per frame and that everything moves in the same frame (#211)
- The two per-frame page preloads (adblock and tab volume) are minified when built, so every frame parses less (#212)

## Packaging
- Apple silicon zip only, unsigned and not notarized. If macOS reports the app is damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Surffish.app` on a copy downloaded from the official release
