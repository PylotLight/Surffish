# Surffish v0.2.0-beta.9

Surffish now handles fullscreen end to end: pages fill the window, and both native and video fullscreen hide the toolbar, sidebar, and window controls.

## New

- **Sidebar layout**: the "Sidebar pushes page" toggle and the "Animation" (resize/slide) select are now one **Layout** control in **Settings → Sidebar & bookmarks bar**, with **Resize**, **Slide**, and **Float** options (Float overlays the page without shifting it).

## Fixed

- Fullscreen pages now fill the window instead of leaving the toolbar inset (#343).
- The toolbar and sidebar stay hidden during video fullscreen (#342).
- The macOS window controls are hidden in native fullscreen (#341).
- Native fullscreen shows the page content only (#340).
- The sidebar rail is hidden during fullscreen and its hover is stabilized (#339).
- The sidebar's hover-close guard holds during native motion (#338).
- When a page's video expands to fill the window, the sidebar rail collapses and the page stays clickable.

## Packaging

- Still unsigned and not notarized. If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/Surffish.app` on a copy you got from the official release.
