# Quick Links background photos

Sockfish bundles four landscape images locally and rotates among them in the Quick Links page. The files are WebP re-encodings of 1920-pixel Wikimedia Commons thumbnails; each listed source file is identified by Commons as **CC0 1.0**. Credits are shown in the app when the corresponding background is active. The CC0 deed permits copying, modification, and redistribution without attribution; credits are retained as good practice.

## Bonjourr comparison

[Bonjourr's official background documentation](https://bonjourr.fr/docs/customisation/backgrounds/) says its “Bonjourr Daylight” option uses the Unsplash API and four curated collections that vary by time of day; it also documents local files, remote URLs, Unsplash collections/search, and manual skip. Its [quick guide](https://bonjourr.fr/docs/quick-guide/) points users to Local files for personal backgrounds.

Sockfish keeps Bonjourr available as a selectable New Tab page. The built-in Quick Links page uses the locally packaged CC0 files instead: rotation works offline, makes no image-service requests, and does not send browsing history to Bonjourr or an image host.
